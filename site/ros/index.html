How pairs are scored
  • Rule: with two D/STs, each week you start the one projected higher. On a bye the other one plays; if both are on bye that week scores 0 (marked ⚠).
  • Pair pts/wk: the points that rule gives, averaged over the weeks in the window (rest of season = this week through week 17 ; playoffs = weeks 15–17 ). Use it to choose a pair.
  • Gain: pair points minus your D/ST on its own (or, for overall pairs and the heatmap's Pairing gain, minus the better of the two on its own). It measures how well the schedules cover each other: byes and weak matchups that fall in different weeks.
  • Uncertainty: weeks after next use projected lines (italic), so they are rougher; the projections already pull far-off weeks toward average, which keeps distant gains small. The page rebuilds daily as lines post.
  • Not available: click teams in the row of buttons to hide D/STs that are rostered in your league; the page remembers them on this device.

How far ahead it can see (back-test 2021–25, ranking accuracy kept vs knowing the real lines): D/ST 85% one week out, 76% at four weeks, 65% at eight; kickers 85%, 82%, 82%. Sorting by points scored so far keeps much less (D/ST weekly rank correlation ≈ 0.08 vs ≈ 0.28 for this page). The Weeks 15–17 column is a rough guide this early and firms up from about week 10.

How this works
  • Models: the weekly D/ST (ESPN, Yahoo, FFPC) and kicker (ESPN, decimal) blends, fed each future matchup. Team, opponent, QB and kicker rates are as of the last game played, so they don't change between now and a future week.
  • Lines: this week = the latest sportsbook consensus; next week = the lookahead lines books have posted (or nflverse's); later weeks = projected. Every posted line gives two implied team totals; a ridge fit of implied total = league average + team offense + opponent defense + home field (recent weeks count most) projects any future game. In the 2021–25 back-test the projected implied totals missed the eventual closing line by 2.1 points one week out, 2.6 at four weeks and 3.1 at eight (3.7 with no team information).
  • Weather: this week's Open-Meteo forecast where available, otherwise the 2016–25 typical weather for that stadium, date (±10 days) and kickoff hour (chances of cold and strong wind enter the models as probabilities). Domes and retractable roofs count as indoor.
  • Columns: Next 3 / Next 4 = average points per game over the next 3 / 4 weeks including this one (* = a bye in that span, so the average is over the games played); ROS avg = average over the remaining games; Wk 15–17 = fantasy playoff total.
  • Not included: future injuries, QB or kicker changes, coaching changes. The page rebuilds every day with new lines; the models refit every Tuesday.
