DraftKings Millionaire 150 lineups — 2026 week 9

Built Sun Nov 8 5:43 AM ET; rebuilt at every refresh until the first kickoff, then frozen. For the DraftKings NFL Millionaire Maker ($20, up to 150 entries, $1M to 1st). Not entered yet: tracked on the Track record tab as if it were ($3,000 a week). The 150: first 90 of the best projections with a QB stack (QB + at least one of his WR / TE) and nobody facing our own D/ST, then 38 Max-ceiling-recipe lineups (QB + 3 + a bring-back, at least $49.9K, aimed at each player's 90th percentile) with the highest ceilings, then the rest from the best projections again; every lineup differs from every other by at least 3 players. Chosen in back-tests on 58 real 2023–26 Millionaires (150 lineups a week, real finishes and payouts, payouts capped at $10K per lineup so one jackpot can't decide it; 380 builds tested): about 56 of 150 cashed a week vs 40 for the high-ceiling build scaled to 150 (clear), capped ROI about +11% vs −8% (likely, not proven), a top-0.1% finish in 15 of 58 weeks. Leaning on ownership fades, exposure caps, QB caps, more differences or salary floors all did worse. Odds above = shares of 10,000 simulated weeks (optimistic in absolute terms). Copies of these lineups entered by others aren't counted yet (being checked).
