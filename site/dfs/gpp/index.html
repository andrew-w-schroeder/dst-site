Built Wed Oct 7 9:55 PM ET; rebuilt at every refresh until the first kickoff, then frozen. Built for one large tournament (the NFL $700K Play-Action: 277,447 entries, 20 max), where the money is in the top 0.01% (about the top 28). Each lineup's P(top 0.01%) = the share of 10,000 simulated weeks in which it beats all but 0.01% of a simulated field of 20,000 lineups drawn from projected ownership (it must be the field's best or 2nd best). The 20 were picked one at a time, each adding the most simulated weeks in which at least one of them gets there, so they win in different weeks; each differs by at least 3 players and no player is in more than half. Ownership matters through the field: a player the field owns at 40% lifts 40% of the field when he hits, so he barely moves you up, while a 3%-owned player who hits jumps you past almost everyone. Candidates (2,664) include lineups that are best in one simulated week and builds that pay for ownership; lineups that copies in the field would share a prize with count for less.
