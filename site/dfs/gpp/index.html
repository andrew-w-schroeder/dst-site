For the top of big tournaments (Play-Action, Millionaire). 2,920 candidate lineups (projection with random noise, random stack rules, some with a $49.9K salary floor or a light ownership discount, never a player facing our own D/ST) are ranked by the trait model: which lineup traits finished in the top 1% more often at the same projection in 61 real 2023–26 Play-Action and Millionaire slates (QB stacks, 3+ receivers most; lower total ownership; never against our own D/ST). The 20 best, each differing by at least 3 players. Back-test (each season ranked by a model fitted on the others): a top-0.1% finish in 7 of 116 contest-weeks vs 0 for the best projections, about 1–1.5 fewer cashes per 20, the same money. The card's ×N = the lineup's top-1% odds vs a typical candidate's. P(cash) / P(top 1%) / P(top 0.01%) = shares of 10,000 simulated weeks against a field of 20,000 lineups drawn from projected ownership.
