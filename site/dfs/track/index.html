DFS lineup track record — 2026

ROI = winnings ÷ entry fees − 1, per tab and contest: 20 entries in the Play-Action ($3) and in the Millionaire ($20) for the three tournament tabs, 10 double-ups for the cash tab. live = the lineups the page froze at that slate's first kickoff (from week 8); backfill = earlier weeks rebuilt with today's code from that week's pre-game data, with models fitted without that season (so it is what the tabs would have done, not what was posted). Finishes and payouts come from the real contests (stat-api): exact payouts in the top ~1,000 places, then a smooth line to the real min cash. Double-ups: the real cash line and payout of DK NFL GIANT Double Ups ($5 / $10 / $25, averaged) where stat-api has them (2026); earlier slates use the Play-Action score at the top 30%, where the real double-up lines sit (GIANT fields are much sharper than Play-Action), paid 2×. Updated Nov 10.

Showdown = 20 lineups in each game's flagship Play-Action ($3, the largest "NFL Showdown $___K Play-Action [20 Entry Max]"), built the way the Showdown tab builds them (the 20 best projections with no player lacking props / a model, any salary left unused, at least 2 of 6 slots different); HP = Highest projected (that build), CS = Captain spread (every QB / RB / WR / TE projected 8.5+ DK points captains at least one lineup, the rest the same build); a week's cell adds up all of that week's games. backfill = every flagship game (primetime or not) through week 8, with projections fitted without that season; live (from week 9) = the Showdown tab's lineups frozen at kickoff, which it posts for primetime games and games alone in their kickoff slot. Scored on the real contest the same way. Updated Nov 10.

Millionaire 150 = the Millionaire (150) tab's build as if entered: 150 lineups in the $20 Millionaire Maker ($3,000 a week; not actually entered). backfill = earlier weeks rebuilt the same way from that week's pre-game data (models fitted without that season) through week 8; live = the tab's lineups frozen at the first kickoff. Real finishes and payouts (our 150 take separate places). Updated Nov 10.
